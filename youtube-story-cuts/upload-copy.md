## 1. Ryan Roberts (long-form, 9:58)

**Title:** The Phone Call at Lunch That Changed My Life

**Description:**

Please note: this story includes the death of a spouse by violence, described plainly.

It was the first day back after the holidays. Ryan almost stayed home. His knees hurt, it was ten below, and he wanted one more day. Instead he kissed his wife goodbye and went to work.

He remembers standing on a rooftop that morning, knee deep in snow, feeling more at peace than he ever had. Then his phone rang at lunch. It was his wife's job. And it wasn't her on the other end of the line.

This is part of Ryan's conversation on The Life Shift. In the full episode, he talks about the twenty years that followed, the grief he could not name, the addiction that nearly took his life, and the long road back.

Full episode: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zqeUo_SyioQ

Ryan Roberts is a father of four and the author of Fragmented: The Crossroads of Trauma, Addiction and Recovery. He has been sober since February 16, 2018.

## 2. Robin Rosenbluth (long-form, 12:06)

**Title:** Raising My Little Sister at 11 on $10 a Week

**Description:**

Robin was eight when her mother died. By eleven, she was the one holding the house together. Her father stayed at his girlfriend's place and left her ten dollars a week to feed herself and her little sister.

Then a family court gave him a choice. Let his girls live with the aunt who wanted them, or send them into foster care. He chose foster care.

In this part of her conversation on The Life Shift, Robin talks about the children's shelter, the friend who was sent back for the color of her skin, and five and a half years without a single word from her family.

Full episode: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1G9amHFQnJM

Robin Rosenbluth is a former foster teen and the author of the memoir Living With Strangers: Healing a Wounded Heart in a Distant Land (She Writes Press, October 27, 2026). She sits on the board of You Gotta Believe, which finds permanent homes for older foster youth.
Website: https://robinrosenbluth.com
